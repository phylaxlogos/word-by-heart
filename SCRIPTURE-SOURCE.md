# Scripture source and attribution

Word by Heart includes the supplied **New American Standard Bible 1995 (NASB95)** text from the official CrossWire **NASB1995 1.2** module.

- [Original CrossWire module download](https://ftp.crosswire.org/ftpmirror/pub/sword/lockmanpackages/NASB1995.zip)

The local conversion contains 31,102 verses across 66 books and 1,189 chapters. Readable text was converted using SWORD 1.9.0, and original OSIS markup is retained separately. Chapter counts were checked against the SWORD versification manifest; this was not a word-by-word comparison with a printed Bible.
